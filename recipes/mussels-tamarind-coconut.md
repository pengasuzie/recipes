## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp groundnut oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1/2 tsp yellow mustard seeds
- 2 hot red chillies, finely chopped
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 400g tomatoes, chopped
- 80ml water
- 1 tbsp tamarind paste, plus extra to taste
- 200ml coconut milk
- 1kg mussels, scrubbed and debearded
- Salt and black pepper
- Coriander or mint leaves, optional

## Method

1. Warm the groundnut oil in a deep, non-stick pan. Add the onion and cook until soft and beginning to colour.
2. Add the mustard seeds, chillies, turmeric, cumin and coriander. Stir for about 1 minute, until fragrant.
3. Add the tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes. Pour in the water, tamarind paste and coconut milk, then bring to the boil. Reduce to a simmer and cook for 5–15 minutes: keep it brief for a soupy sauce or cook longer for a thicker one.
4. Add the mussels and season with salt and black pepper. Cover and cook for 3–4 minutes, until the shells open. Discard any mussels that remain closed.
5. Scatter over coriander or mint, if using, and serve with rice or bread.

## My notes

- Clams, shell-on prawns, salmon, cod or haddock can be used instead of the mussels.
- Add more tamarind paste, 1 tsp at a time, for a sharper sauce.
