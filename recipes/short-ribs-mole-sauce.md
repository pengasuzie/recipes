Fall-apart short ribs combining a Mexican-inspired chilli and cocoa mole profile with the watercress finish of Brazilian rabada com agrião.

## Ingredients

- 4 individual bone-in beef short ribs (about 2kg)
- 2 tbsp sunflower oil
- 2 onions, halved and thinly sliced
- 6 garlic cloves, crushed with the side of a knife
- 2 tomatoes, finely chopped (about 240g)
- 1 tsp fine salt
- 2 tbsp red pepper paste or tomato paste
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 litre chicken or beef stock, with some water if preferred
- 200ml pure pomegranate juice
- 80g watercress

### Mole rub

- 2 dried ancho chillies, stalks removed and torn in half
- 1 dried cascabel chilli, stalk removed and torn in half
- 1 dried chipotle chilli, stalk and seeds removed and torn in half
- 4 cinnamon sticks, roughly broken
- 1 tbsp cocoa powder
- 2 tsp black peppercorns, roughly crushed
- 1 tbsp fine salt

## Method

1. Blend or pound all the mole-rub ingredients into a coarse powder.
2. Trim the fat and silver skin from the meaty side of the ribs, leaving the bone side intact. Pat dry and rub each rib all over with 1 tablespoon of mole rub.
3. Heat the sunflower oil in a large lidded casserole over high heat. Sear the ribs, bone-side up, on each of the three long meaty sides for 4–5 minutes per side, until deeply browned. Work in batches if needed, then set aside.
4. Heat the oven to 140°C fan (160°C conventional). Lower the casserole to medium-high heat, add the onions and garlic and fry for 7 minutes, stirring often, until golden and soft.
5. Add the tomatoes and salt and cook for 2 minutes. Stir in the pepper paste and Worcestershire sauce and cook for 1 minute more.
6. Add the stock, pomegranate juice and remaining mole rub. Return the ribs to the pan bone-side up.
7. Cover and braise for 4–5 hours, until the meat is extremely tender. Check occasionally that the meat remains submerged and the bones face upwards.
8. Rest the ribs in the covered pan for 20–30 minutes. Transfer them to a tray and skim as much fat as possible from the sauce.
9. Warm the sauce, remove it from the heat and stir through the watercress. Return the ribs to the pan and serve.

## My notes

- Serves 4–6. Good with polenta, mash, corn tortillas or brown-butter curried cornbread.
- Other mild or medium dried chillies can replace the ancho, cascabel and chipotle.
