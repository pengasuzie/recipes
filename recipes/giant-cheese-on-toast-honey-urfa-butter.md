Giant Cheese on Toast with Green Onion, Honey and Urfa Butter by Ixta Belfrage from her cookbook Mezcla.

## Ingredients

- 1 loaf sourdough or focaccia
- 300g mature Cheddar, roughly grated
- 150g double cream
- 50g Greek yoghurt
- 1 jalapeño or green chilli (optional), very thinly sliced
- 1/2 onion, very thinly sliced on a mandoline
- Flaked sea salt

### Spring onion butter

- 50g cold unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 plump garlic cloves, peeled and finely chopped
- 2–3 spring onions, green ends finely sliced into rounds
- 25g floral honey (Manuka if you have it)
- 3/4 tsp Urfa chilli flakes
- 1/4 tsp fine salt

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 250°C (230°C fan).
2. Halve the bread widthways. Place the bottom half, cut-side up, on a flat baking tray. Once the oven is hot, bake for 5 minutes, until the surface is golden and lightly toasted. Save the other half for another use, or double the toppings to make both halves.
3. Mix the cheese, cream and yoghurt in a medium bowl. Spread the cheese mixture all over the toasted bread. Return it to the top shelf of the oven for 8–9 minutes, until brown and bubbling. Increase the heat to brown it further if needed.
4. Meanwhile, put all the ingredients for the spring onion butter into a medium saucepan over medium-low heat. Cook for 4 minutes. The garlic should soften without browning or becoming crispy, so reduce the heat if needed.
5. Spoon the butter over the cheesy bread, then finish with the chilli and onion. Return it to the oven for 2 minutes.
6. Sprinkle with flaked salt and serve.

## My notes

- Naina used Gruyère instead of Cheddar and ciabatta instead of sourdough.
