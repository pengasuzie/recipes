A whole sea bass roasted in citrus butter, then wrapped in lettuce with soft herbs and a bright tangerine dipping sauce.

## Ingredients

- 1 whole sea bass (about 800g), at room temperature
- 2 rosemary stalks
- 4 thin spring onions (about 40g)
- Fine salt
- 1 baby gem lettuce, leaves separated
- 5g mint leaves
- 5g coriander leaves
- 5g basil leaves
- Lime wedges, to serve

### Citrus butter

- 40g unsalted butter
- 30ml olive oil
- 1 1/2 tbsp tangerine juice
- 2 tsp lime juice
- 3 garlic cloves, unpeeled and crushed with the side of a knife
- 1 tsp fine salt
- About 20 twists black pepper

### Tangerine dipping sauce

- 1 1/2 tsp pul biber, or milder red pepper flakes
- 1 small garlic clove, finely chopped
- 1/4 tsp finely grated ginger
- 3/4 tsp tomato paste
- 1/4 tsp fine salt
- 1 1/2 tbsp mild olive oil
- 40g sweet cherry tomatoes, very finely chopped
- 4 tbsp tangerine juice
- 2 tsp lime juice
- 1/4 tsp fish sauce or light soy sauce

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 190°C fan (210°C conventional). Pat the fish very dry and set it diagonally in a snug, high-sided baking tray.
2. Cut five diagonal slashes in the fish. Season the cavity with 1/4 teaspoon salt, then stuff it with the rosemary and spring onions.
3. Melt all the citrus butter ingredients together in a small saucepan over medium heat. Pour the mixture over and around the fish.
4. Bake for 14 minutes, or a little longer for a larger fish. Baste well, switch the oven to its hottest grill setting and grill on the top shelf for 4–6 minutes, watching closely, until the skin bubbles and crisps.
5. While the fish cooks, pound the pul biber, garlic, ginger, tomato paste and salt to a rough paste with a mortar and pestle.
6. Heat the dipping-sauce oil for 1 minute over medium heat, pour it onto the paste and stir. Mix in the tomatoes, tangerine juice, lime juice and fish sauce.
7. Move the fish and browned citrus butter to a platter and discard the garlic skins. Serve with lettuce, herbs, lime wedges and the dipping sauce for making parcels.

## My notes

- The complete dish uses roughly 4 tangerines and 2 limes.
