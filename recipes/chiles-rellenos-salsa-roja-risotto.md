Charred peppers stuffed with bubbling feta and mozzarella, served over brown rice loosened with a smooth, spicy tomato salsa.

## Ingredients

- 8 red Romano peppers
- 200g short-grain brown rice, soaked for at least 2 hours or overnight
- 300g feta, broken into small pieces
- 200g buffalo mozzarella, well drained, patted dry and torn into chunks
- 2 spring onions, finely julienned
- 5g coriander leaves
- 2 limes, cut into wedges
- Olive oil and salt, as needed

### Salsa roja

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 15g unsalted butter
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped (about 120g)
- 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 300g sweet cherry tomatoes
- 1/2 dried ancho chilli
- 1 dried habanero, or a pinch of chilli flakes for less heat
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp coriander seeds
- 1 tsp fine salt
- 120ml water
- 2 tsp tomato paste

## Method

1. Heat the grill to its highest setting. Cut a lengthwise slit in each pepper, leaving it attached at both ends, and place slit-side up on a rack over a tray.
2. Grill for about 7 minutes, turn, then grill for another 4–8 minutes until softened and blackened in patches. Cool slit-side down so the liquid drains.
3. Drain the rice and combine it with 450ml water in a saucepan. Bring to a simmer, cover and cook over low heat for 25 minutes. Remove from the heat and rest, covered, for 10 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, put the salsa ingredients except the water and tomato paste in a large sauté pan. Fry over medium-high heat for about 15 minutes, stirring often, until the tomatoes collapse and the onion is golden and soft.
5. Remove the habanero, squeezing it into the mixture first if more heat is wanted. Blend the salsa with the water and tomato paste until smooth, return it to the pan and set aside.
6. Mix the feta and mozzarella. Peel away only the loose skin from the cooled peppers, pat the insides dry, season lightly and fill with the cheese. Place on a foil-lined tray, drizzle with oil and add a little flaked salt.
7. Grill the stuffed peppers near the top of the oven for 7–9 minutes, until the cheese bubbles and browns.
8. Stir the cooked rice into the salsa and warm gently for a minute. Add a splash of water if needed for a loose risotto consistency.
9. Spread the rice on a platter, top with the peppers, spring onions and coriander, and finish with lime juice.

## My notes

- Fresh poblano chillies can replace the Romano peppers.
- The peppers can be stuffed up to 4 hours ahead, then grilled just before serving.
