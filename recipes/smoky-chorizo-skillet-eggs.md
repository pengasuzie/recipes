A fast, smoky skillet of eggs, chorizo and golden cherry tomatoes, finished with cumin, chilli, fresh basil and plenty of lemon.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 100g cooking chorizo, sliced into rounds
- 200g yellow cherry tomatoes
- 1 garlic clove, finely sliced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp cumin seeds, lightly crushed
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes, plus extra to serve
- 60ml water
- 3 eggs
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1 handful basil leaves
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Warm flatbread or crusty bread, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a medium cast-iron or non-stick frying pan over medium heat. Add the chorizo and cook for 2–3 minutes, turning once, until browned and its oil has rendered into the pan.
2. Add the cherry tomatoes and cook for 3–4 minutes, shaking the pan occasionally, until glossy and beginning to soften.
3. Stir in the garlic, tomato paste, smoked paprika, half the cumin seeds and half the chilli flakes. Cook for 1 minute, then add the water and stir to make a loose, smoky sauce. Simmer for 2 minutes and season lightly with salt and pepper.
4. Make three spaces in the sauce and crack in the eggs. Cover the pan and cook over medium-low heat for 4–6 minutes, until the whites are set but the yolks remain soft.
5. Scatter over the remaining cumin seeds and chilli flakes, then finish with the basil leaves. Serve immediately from the pan with lemon wedges and warm flatbread or crusty bread.
