A punchy one-pan bake of crisp chicken thighs, sweet pineapple, smoky chilli and tangerine.

## Ingredients

- 4 skin-on, bone-in chicken thighs, at room temperature
- 4 garlic cloves, peeled and crushed with the side of a knife
- 1 medium onion, halved and very thinly sliced
- 1/2 large extra-ripe pineapple, peeled (about 300g)
- 100ml fresh tangerine juice (from about 4 tangerines), or orange juice
- 100ml chicken stock or water
- 2 tbsp double cream
- 5g coriander leaves
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

### 'Nduja and chipotle paste

- 50g 'nduja paste
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp tomato paste
- 1/2 tsp chipotle flakes
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 3/4 tsp fine salt
- About 20 twists black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C fan (200°C conventional).
2. Mix all the paste ingredients in a large bowl. Add the chicken, garlic and three-quarters of the onion and turn until evenly coated.
3. Spread the onion and garlic in a 28cm ovenproof frying pan or similar baking dish. Arrange the chicken on top, skin-side up and spaced apart.
4. Cut the pineapple into four rounds, remove the hard core and quarter each round. Toss the pieces through the paste left in the bowl, then nestle them around the chicken.
5. Pour the tangerine juice around the chicken without wetting the skin. Bake for 20 minutes.
6. Pour the stock around the chicken, again keeping the skin dry. Bake for another 20–25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the skin is browned and crisp.
7. Drizzle the cream into the pan sauce. Toss the coriander and remaining onion with a little oil and salt, scatter over the bake and serve with lime wedges.

## My notes

- The chicken can marinate in the paste for up to 2 days; add the onion and garlic only when ready to bake.
- If the citrus is sharp rather than sweet, add a little maple syrup or honey with the stock.
