Thomasina Miers' aromatic mussels in a rich coconut and turmeric sauce, served with jasmine rice and an optional curry-leaf temper. Photograph by Yuki Sugiura for The Guardian; food styling by Aya Nishimura.

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp rapeseed or groundnut oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- Salt and black pepper
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 large knob ginger, finely chopped
- 2 bird's eye chillies, finely chopped
- 1 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tbsp ground coriander
- 2 x 400ml tins coconut milk
- 150ml water
- 2 tbsp fish sauce, or to taste
- 375g jasmine rice
- 650ml cold water
- 1.5kg mussels, cleaned and debearded

### Curry-leaf temper (optional)

- 3 tbsp rapeseed oil
- 2 heaped tsp black mustard seeds
- 16–18 curry leaves

## Method

1. Warm 2 tbsp of the oil in a medium pan over medium heat. Add the onion, season with salt and cook for 10 minutes, until soft and coloured.
2. Add the garlic, ginger and chillies and cook for 3 minutes. Stir in the turmeric and coriander and cook for another minute.
3. Pour in the coconut milk and 150ml water. Season to taste with fish sauce, bring to a simmer and cook very gently while preparing the rice.
4. Rinse the rice once under cold running water, then put it in a saucepan with 650ml cold water. Bring to the boil, cover and reduce the heat to its lowest setting. Cook undisturbed for 10 minutes, then remove from the heat and keep covered somewhere warm.
5. Heat the remaining 1 tbsp oil in a lidded wok or large pan over high heat. When smoking, add the mussels, shake the pan once and cover. Cook for 1 minute.
6. Pour in the coconut sauce and cook for a few minutes, until the sauce is simmering and the mussels have opened. Discard any that remain closed.
7. For the temper, warm the rapeseed oil in a small frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the mustard seeds; as soon as they pop, add the curry leaves and cook until crisp and translucent. Remove from the heat.
8. Fluff the rice with a fork and divide it between bowls. Ladle over the mussels and sauce, then finish with the temper.

## My notes

- Fresh curry leaves freeze well.
- The coconut and turmeric sauce also works with fish, potatoes and spinach, or cauliflower.
