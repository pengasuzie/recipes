Stephanie Alexander's simple fried eggs with garlic and lemon, seasoned with cumin and fresh mint and served on warm flatbread.

## Ingredients

- 20g butter
- 1 garlic clove, finely sliced
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 eggs
- Freshly ground cumin seeds
- 1 slice hot toast or flatbread
- Salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- A few mint leaves, shredded

## Method

1. Melt the butter over medium-low heat. When it starts to foam, add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds, until just starting to change colour.
2. Add the lemon juice and eggs, then fry gently until the eggs are set to your liking.
3. Sprinkle over a good pinch of cumin and serve on the hot toast or flatbread. Season with salt and pepper, then finish with the shredded mint.
