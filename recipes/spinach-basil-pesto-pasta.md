A loose, punchy spinach-and-basil pesto pasta, finished with fresh ricotta and extra Parmesan.

## Ingredients

- ½ large bag or 1 small bag baby spinach
- A generous amount of fresh basil
- At least ½ cup (125ml) extra virgin olive oil, plus more as needed
- 1 generous handful mixed nuts, such as skin-on almonds and pine nuts
- 1 small garlic clove
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika or sweet paprika
- Chilli flakes or chilli powder, to taste
- Juice of 1 lemon, or a generous splash of red wine vinegar
- ½ block Parmesan, roughly grated
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 400g pasta
- Pasta cooking water, as needed
- Fresh ricotta, to serve

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to the boil and cook the pasta until al dente. Reserve a mugful of the cooking water before draining.
2. Meanwhile, add the spinach, basil, 125ml olive oil, nuts, garlic, paprika, chilli, lemon juice or vinegar, Parmesan, a little salt and some pepper to a blender.
3. Blend until smooth, adding more olive oil until the pesto reaches the consistency you like. If serving straight away, blend in a splash of the reserved pasta water to loosen and emulsify it.
4. Toss the pesto through the hot drained pasta, adding another splash of pasta water if needed.
5. Divide between bowls and spoon or crumble fresh ricotta over the top.

## My notes

- Almonds with the skin on are especially good. Add pine nuts as well if you have them.
- The olive oil quantity is a starting point; keep adding it until the pesto looks right.
