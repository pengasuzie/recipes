A Brazilian-style condensed milk flan sharpened with fresh passion fruit and a little tangerine zest.

## Ingredients

- 100g caster sugar
- 5 large passion fruits (about 90g pulp)
- 397g tin condensed milk
- 320ml whole milk
- 1 1/2 tsp finely grated tangerine zest
- 1/4 tsp chilli flakes, optional
- 2 large eggs, plus 1 egg yolk
- 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
- Pinch of fine salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 150°C fan. Set out a solid 20cm round tin or similar ovenproof dish; do not use a loose-bottomed tin.
2. Sprinkle the sugar evenly into a medium non-stick frying pan. Cook over medium heat for 5–6 minutes, swirling once it starts to melt, until the caramel becomes dark amber.
3. Immediately pour the caramel into the tin and swirl to cover the base. Set aside to harden.
4. Scoop the passion fruit pulp into a large bowl. Add the condensed milk, whole milk, zest, chilli flakes if using, eggs, yolk, vanilla and salt. Whisk gently until combined without introducing too many bubbles.
5. Put the pudim tin inside a large, high-sided baking tray. Strain the custard into the tin.
6. Place the tray on the middle oven shelf, then pour hot—not boiling—water into the outer tray until it reaches halfway up the pudim tin.
7. Bake for 45 minutes, until set on top but still wobbly. Cool for 1 hour, remove from the water bath and refrigerate overnight.
8. Take the pudim from the fridge 10 minutes before serving. Run a knife around the edge, cover with a large lipped platter and invert quickly. Lift away the tin, slice and serve with the caramel sauce.

## My notes

- The overnight chill is additional to the listed time.
