Turmeric-roasted chicken with crisp-edged potatoes, silky red onions and pops of cumin, coriander and mustard seed.

## Ingredients

- 1.25kg roasting potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
- 1½ teaspoons ground turmeric
- 6 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon plain flour
- 2 teaspoons cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon black mustard seeds
- 2 tablespoons coriander seeds
- ½ teaspoon chilli powder
- 1 thumb-sized piece fresh ginger, peeled and roughly chopped
- 1–2 green chillies, roughly chopped
- 3 red onions, peeled and finely sliced
- 4 chicken drumsticks
- 8 chicken thighs
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Coriander leaves, to serve
- 1 lemon, half juiced and half cut into wedges

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C, or 200°C fan. Put the potatoes in a deep saucepan with ½ teaspoon turmeric and ½ teaspoon sea salt, cover with cold water and bring to the boil. Simmer for 10–15 minutes, until just tender when pierced with a knife.
2. While the potatoes cook, pour 5 tablespoons vegetable oil into a large baking tray and place it in the oven to heat.
3. Drain the potatoes, then toss them with the flour, cumin seeds, mustard seeds, coriander seeds, chilli powder and plenty of salt and pepper. Add the ginger, green chilli and red onion, bashing the potato edges slightly as everything is mixed.
4. Carefully tip the potato mixture into the hot oil and turn to coat. Roast for 25 minutes.
5. Rub the chicken with the remaining tablespoon of oil, the remaining teaspoon of turmeric, the garlic and a generous seasoning of salt and pepper.
6. Reduce the oven to 200°C, or 180°C fan. Arrange the chicken skin-side up over the potatoes and roast for a further 30 minutes. Turn the potatoes once or twice so they crisp evenly; the chicken is ready when its skin is crisp and its juices run clear.
7. Scatter over the coriander leaves, squeeze over the lemon juice and serve with the lemon wedges.

## My notes

- Serve with raita and warm flatbreads to catch the spiced oil.
- For a meat-free version, roast the potatoes without the chicken and add broccoli or asparagus for the final 20 minutes, or stir through cooked peas at the end.
