Crisp-edged Savoy cabbage over a warm, sweet and spicy mango-harissa sauce.

## Ingredients

### Cabbage

- 1 medium Savoy cabbage
- 1 tsp flaked salt
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Lemon wedges, to serve
- 1 jalapeño, thinly sliced
- 5g fresh dill and coriander leaves
- Greek-style yoghurt, to serve (optional)

### Mango and harissa sauce

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 large garlic cloves, very finely chopped
- 1 mild red chilli, deseeded and very finely chopped
- 1/8 tsp fine salt
- 2 tbsp rose harissa
- 2 tsp tomato paste
- 100g very ripe mango flesh, cut into 1cm cubes
- 2 tsp lemon juice

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C fan. Separate about 400g of the brightest green cabbage leaves, reserving the rest for another dish.
2. Cut each leaf in half along the rib and then in half again. Massage with the flaked salt and olive oil in a large bowl.
3. Spread the leaves over a large, flat tray and roast for 12 minutes, tossing halfway, until the edges brown and crisp.
4. Meanwhile, combine the sauce oil, garlic, chilli and fine salt in a large sauté pan. Cook over medium-low heat for 6–7 minutes, stirring often, until the garlic is very soft but not browned.
5. Take the pan from the heat. Stir in the harissa and tomato paste, then fold in the mango and lemon juice.
6. Spoon the warm sauce over a platter and pile the roast cabbage on top. Finish with lemon juice, jalapeño and herbs, and serve with yoghurt if wanted.

## My notes

- The sauce also works with roast chicken, tofu, grilled fish or prawns.
