Mussels and orzo steamed in a light coconut broth scented with saffron, Scotch bonnet and lemon.

## Ingredients

- 1kg mussels, scrubbed and debearded
- 80g orzo
- 30g unsalted butter
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 small onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, very finely chopped
- 1 yellow capsicum, cut into 1cm pieces
- 1 whole Scotch bonnet chilli
- 1 1/4 tsp fine salt
- 150g yellow cherry tomatoes, roughly chopped
- 1/2 tsp tomato paste
- 3/4 tsp saffron threads
- 400ml coconut milk
- Up to 2 tsp chilli paste, optional
- 1 lemon

### Herb oil

- 5g chives, finely chopped
- 5g coriander, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Pinch of fine salt

## Method

1. Cover the orzo with boiling water in a medium bowl. Soak for 15 minutes, then drain and rinse.
2. Melt the butter with the olive oil in a large, shallow sauté pan over medium-low heat. Add the onion, garlic, capsicum, whole Scotch bonnet and salt. Cook gently for about 8 minutes, until soft but not coloured.
3. Stir in the tomatoes, tomato paste and saffron and cook for 2 minutes. Add the coconut milk and simmer for 3 minutes.
4. Stir in the drained orzo, then spread the mussels over the top. Cover, reduce the heat to its lowest setting and cook for about 5 minutes, until the mussels open. Discard any that stay closed.
5. Remove the Scotch bonnet, squeezing it into the sauce first only if more heat is wanted. Taste and add chilli paste if needed.
6. Squeeze plenty of lemon juice over the mussels. Mix the herb-oil ingredients and spoon over the stew before serving.

## My notes

- Serve with crusty bread for the coconut-saffron broth.
