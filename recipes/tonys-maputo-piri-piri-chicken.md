Tony's fiery Maputo-style chicken is marinated overnight in garlic, chillies, capsicum, lemon and vinegar, then cooked over hot coals and drenched in piri-piri sauce.

## Ingredients

### Chicken and marinade

- 1 large chicken, quartered or cut into 8 pieces
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 10 garlic cloves
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 1 habanero or other hot chilli, seeds included
- 1 handful mixed whole chillies
- 1 red capsicum, deseeded and roughly chopped
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2 cups (500ml) neutral cooking oil
- ½ cup (125ml) red wine vinegar
- Chilli powder, to taste (optional)

### To serve

- Thick-cut, double-fried potato chips
- Lemon wedges

## Method

1. Put the salt, garlic, paprika, habanero, mixed chillies, capsicum, lemon juice, oil and red wine vinegar in a blender. Blend until smooth.
2. Arrange the chicken pieces in a non-reactive dish and pour over the marinade, turning to coat well. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
3. Prepare a charcoal barbecue for direct cooking over hot coals. Remove the chicken from the marinade and sprinkle with chilli powder, if using.
4. Barbecue the chicken, turning regularly and basting a few times with the marinade. Cook until well browned and the thickest part of each piece reaches 75°C. Stop basting before the chicken finishes cooking so the final coating cooks through.
5. Pour the remaining marinade into a clean frying pan and bring it to a full boil before using it as a sauce.
6. Serve the chicken with double-fried thick-cut chips and lemon wedges, spooning the hot piri-piri sauce over the chicken and chips.

## My notes

- For an easier serving sauce, reserve some freshly blended marinade before it touches the raw chicken, refrigerate it separately, then boil it before serving.
- This is a large batch of marinade; be generous when basting and serving.
