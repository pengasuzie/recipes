## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 1 large chicken

### Marinade

- 2 teaspoons of salt
- 10 garlic cloves
- 1 tablespoon of Paprika
- 1 Habanero/hot chilli, including seeds
- Handful of other chillies whole
- 1 red capsicum
- Juice of a lemon
- 2 cups of ordinary cooking oil
- Half a cup of red wine vinegar

### To serve

- Double fried thick potato fries
- A wedge of lemon

## Method

1. Quarter or eighth a large chicken.
2. Prepare and blend a marinade consisting of the marinade ingredients above.
3. Place chicken pieces in dish, pour marinade over and keep in fridge for min 4 hours, or preferably overnight, before cooking. Sprinkle some chilli powder over chicken before putting on the fire if you like.
4. BBQ chicken pieces on hot coals, basting with excess marinade a few times.
5. Boil the remaining marinade in a fry pan to pour over the served chicken and potatoes/fries.
6. Serve with double fried thick potato fries and a wedge of lemon.
