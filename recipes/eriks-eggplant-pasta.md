## Ingredients

- Extra virgin olive oil
- 2 big fresh garlic bulbs — chop
- 2 eggplants — diced into 2cm cubes and lightly salt in colander for an hour
- Fresh chilli — chop
- 2.5kg "Organic" DICED tomatoes
- 4 DRY chorizos — peel and dice
- 0.5kg Vintage Cheddar
- 1 bunch of fresh basil — chop
- 1kg (160g each avg, enough?) of rigatoni pasta (Garofalo or Barilla, penne also ok)
- 250g Parmesan (Padano or Reggiano, to be grated on top at the end)

## Method

1. Soffritto: warm up the pan, put the oil and when it's hot add the garlic.
2. Add the chorizo and cook 3–5 mins to dry it up a bit.
3. Add the eggplants, likely more oil (eggplants are sponges), cook for another 5–10 mins.
4. Add the tomatoes.
5. Add a little salt, not much (or nothing), the chorizo and the cheddar provides quite some salt by itself.
6. Add the chillies, cook for another 20 mins.
7. Add the shredded cheddar and stir it in.
8. Add the basil and mix with pasta al dente.
