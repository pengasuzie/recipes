A fast vegetarian ragù with the depth of a long-cooked sauce, made from dried porcini, tomato and Parmesan.

## Ingredients

- 40g dried porcini mushrooms
- 55ml olive oil, plus extra to serve
- 3 garlic cloves, very finely chopped
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes, or less to taste
- 10g parsley, stems and leaves finely chopped
- 1/3 tsp fine sea salt
- 1 1/2 tbsp tomato paste
- Black pepper
- 250g dried tagliatelle
- 40g Parmesan, finely grated, plus extra to serve
- 3 tbsp double cream

## Method

1. Cover the porcini with boiling water and soak for 10 minutes. Reserve 75ml of the soaking liquid, drain the mushrooms and chop them finely to a mince-like texture.
2. Bring a large saucepan of salted water to the boil for the pasta.
3. Combine the olive oil, garlic, chilli flakes, parsley and salt in a cold large sauté pan. Cook gently over medium-low heat for 5 minutes, until the garlic is soft and lightly golden.
4. Increase the heat to medium-high. Add the porcini, tomato paste and about 20 twists of black pepper and fry for 3 minutes. Take the pan off the heat.
5. Cook the tagliatelle until al dente. Reserve about 350ml pasta water, then drain.
6. Return the porcini pan to medium-high heat and add the reserved pasta water and mushroom soaking liquid. Simmer for 3 minutes.
7. Stir in half the Parmesan until melted, then the remainder. Reduce the heat to medium and stir in the cream and drained pasta.
8. Toss for about 90 seconds, until the sauce emulsifies and coats the tagliatelle. Serve immediately with olive oil and extra Parmesan.

## My notes

- Serves 2 as a main or 4 as a starter.
