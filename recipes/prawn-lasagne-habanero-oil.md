A rich prawn lasagne with miso-spiked tomato ragù, cream, Parmesan and a smoky habanero oil for a serious chilli kick.

## Ingredients

### Prawn ragù

- 6 tablespoons olive oil
- ½ onion, finely chopped
- 5 garlic cloves, finely grated or crushed
- 200g ripe cherry tomatoes, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon fine salt
- 500g raw peeled king prawns, chopped to a fine mince
- 1 dried habanero chilli, or chilli flakes for less heat
- 80g tomato paste
- 2 teaspoons white miso paste
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 100ml white wine
- 500ml unsalted fish, shellfish or chicken stock

### Habanero oil

- 120ml olive oil
- 2 teaspoons tomato paste
- ½ teaspoon chipotle flakes
- 1 dried habanero chilli, deseeded and finely chopped
- ½ teaspoon sweet paprika
- ¼ teaspoon fine salt

### To assemble

- 250g fresh lasagne sheets
- 130g double cream, plus 2 tablespoons to finish
- 100g Parmesan, finely grated
- 10g fresh chives, finely chopped

## Method

1. For the ragù, heat 4 tablespoons olive oil in a large sauté pan over medium heat. Add the onion, garlic, tomatoes and salt, then cook for 8–10 minutes, stirring often, until the onion is soft and golden and the tomatoes have broken down.
2. Increase the heat to high, add the minced prawns and fry for about 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly coloured.
3. Add the dried habanero, tomato paste, miso and plenty of black pepper. Cook for another 3 minutes, reducing the heat if the mixture begins to catch.
4. Pour in the wine and let it bubble for 30 seconds. Add the stock and remaining 2 tablespoons olive oil, bring to a simmer, then cook gently over medium-low heat for 18–20 minutes. Remove the whole habanero, squeezing it into the sauce first if you want more heat.
5. Meanwhile, make the habanero oil. Combine all its ingredients in a small saucepan over medium heat, whisking to disperse the tomato paste. Heat until gently bubbling, about 1½ minutes, then remove from the heat and set aside.
6. Heat the oven to 220°C, or 200°C fan. In a roughly 20cm square baking dish, lay down enough pasta sheets to cover the base. Top with one-fifth each of the ragù, cream, Parmesan and chives. Repeat to make 5 layers and use all the ingredients.
7. Drizzle ½ tablespoon habanero oil over the lasagne. Cover the dish tightly with foil and bake for 20 minutes.
8. Increase the oven to 250°C, or 230°C fan. Remove the foil and bake for another 10–12 minutes, until the edges are deeply crisp.
9. Rest for 10 minutes, then finish with the extra cream and a generous drizzle of habanero oil before serving.

## My notes

- The habanero oil recipe makes more than you need. Cool it completely and store the remainder in a clean jar in the fridge.
- Starting with shell-on prawns? Allow about 1kg and use the shells to make the stock.
- Reduce the habanero or substitute ordinary chilli flakes for a gentler lasagne.
