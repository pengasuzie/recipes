Pan-charred Turkish flatbreads stuffed with lemony greens and feta.

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 384g gluten-free plain flour or regular plain flour, plus extra for dusting
- ½ tsp salt
- 60ml extra virgin olive oil, plus more for cooking
- About 240ml water, plus more if needed
- ½–1 lemon, cut into wedges, to serve

### Spinach and feta filling

- 1 brown or yellow onion, diced
- 4 cups spinach, finely torn kale leaves, or a mixture
- Juice of ½ lemon
- Sea salt and black pepper
- 170–200g feta, crumbled

## Method

1. Combine the flour, salt and olive oil in a bowl. Stir in about half the water, then gradually add enough of the remainder to form a cohesive dough that is not overly sticky.
2. Turn onto a lightly floured bench and knead for a few minutes until smooth. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, combine the onion, spinach or kale and lemon juice in a large bowl. Season with salt and pepper, add the feta, then massage everything together until evenly mixed. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
4. Divide the rested dough into four equal pieces. On a lightly floured bench, roll each piece into an 18–20cm circle. Mound a quarter of the filling over one half, fold the dough over and press it into a semicircle. It does not need to be sealed tightly.
5. Heat a medium or large frying pan over medium-high heat. Add a drizzle of olive oil and carefully lay in one gozleme. Press it against the pan with a flexible spatula and cook for 3–4 minutes until charred in spots. Flip and cook the second side for another 3–4 minutes. Repeat with the remaining gozleme, adding more oil as needed.
6. Cut into wedges, squeeze over lemon juice and serve immediately.

## My notes

- A mixture of spinach and kale works well; mushrooms can also be added to the filling.
- The feta can be replaced with grated halloumi, cheddar, mozzarella or gruyère.
