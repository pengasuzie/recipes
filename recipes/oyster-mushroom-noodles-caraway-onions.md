Creamy egg noodles with crisp roast oyster mushrooms, deeply caramelised caraway onions and fresh lemony herbs.

## Ingredients

- 200g fresh medium egg noodles, or cooked dried egg noodles
- 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 10g chives, finely chopped
- 10g dill leaves
- Crème fraîche or sour cream, to serve
- Olive oil and black pepper, to serve

### Mushrooms

- 400g oyster mushrooms
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp fine salt

### Sauce

- 40g unsalted butter
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 onions, very finely chopped
- 3/4 tsp caraway seeds
- 1 1/4 tsp fine salt, divided
- 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 500ml chicken or vegetable stock
- 2 1/2 tsp English mustard
- 5 tbsp double cream
- Black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan (220°C conventional). Tear the mushrooms in half and toss them with the olive oil and salt on a large lined tray.
2. Roast for 22–25 minutes, stirring halfway, until golden and beginning to crisp at the edges. Set aside.
3. Meanwhile, combine the butter, sauce oil, onions, caraway seeds and 3/4 teaspoon salt in a large sauté pan. Cook over medium heat for about 12 minutes, stirring regularly, until soft and caramelised.
4. Reduce the heat to its lowest setting and cook for another 5 minutes. The onions should become deep golden-brown without turning crisp or burnt.
5. Reserve half the onions on a plate. Stir the garlic into the pan for 1 minute, then remove the pan from the heat.
6. Add the stock, mustard, cream, remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt and plenty of black pepper. Stir in half the roast mushrooms.
7. Return the pan to medium heat, simmer for 4 minutes, then add the noodles and cook for about 3 minutes, until hot and the sauce has thickened slightly.
8. Stir in the lemon juice and half the herbs. Transfer to a warm platter and top with the reserved mushrooms, onions and herbs, plus the lemon zest.
9. Finish with crème fraîche, a little olive oil and black pepper.

## My notes

- The sauce can be made ahead and reheated before adding the noodles.
