Juicy tomatoes and cucumber in a ginger-tahini dressing, topped with chilli oil and deeply crisp crumpet croutons.

## Ingredients

- 1 large cucumber, peeled, halved and watery centre scraped out (about 260g)
- 5 ripe plum tomatoes, deseeded and cut into 1.5–2cm cubes (about 360g prepared)
- 1/3 tsp fine salt
- 1/2 large lime

### Crumpet croutons

- 3 crumpets (about 160g), cut into 2.5cm chunks
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large garlic clove, finely grated
- 1/4 tsp fine salt
- Black pepper

### Chilli oil

- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large garlic clove, very finely chopped
- 1 1/2 tsp tomato paste
- 1 1/4 tsp pul biber or Aleppo chilli flakes, plus extra to serve
- 1/4 tsp Urfa chilli flakes, plus extra to serve
- 1/8 tsp fine salt

### Tahini ginger sauce

- 60g tahini, well stirred
- 1 tsp finely grated ginger
- 1 1/2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1/2 tbsp water
- 1 1/2 tbsp maple syrup

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C fan (200°C conventional).
2. Cut the cucumber into 1.5–2cm cubes. Combine it with the tomatoes and salt in a bowl, crush very lightly with your hands and set aside.
3. Toss the crumpet pieces with their oil, garlic, salt and plenty of pepper on a flat tray. Bake for about 14 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crisp. Leave to cool.
4. For the chilli oil, heat the olive oil in a small saucepan over medium heat for 90 seconds. Remove from the heat and whisk in the garlic, tomato paste, both chilli flakes and salt.
5. Whisk all the tahini sauce ingredients in a medium bowl until smooth.
6. Fold the tahini sauce and one-third of the chilli oil through the cucumber and tomatoes, leaving visible streaks of oil rather than overmixing.
7. Spoon onto a lipped platter and squeeze over the lime. Finish with extra chilli flakes, the remaining chilli oil and the crumpet croutons.

## My notes

- The tahini sauce keeps refrigerated for 3 days; the chilli oil keeps for up to 3 weeks.
