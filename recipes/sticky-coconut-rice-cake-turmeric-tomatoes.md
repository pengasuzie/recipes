A hands-off coconut sticky-rice cake with crisp edges, served with jammy turmeric and cumin tomatoes.

## Ingredients

### Rice cake

- 400g Thai sticky rice, rinsed and drained
- 400ml full-fat coconut milk
- 250ml water
- 2 small garlic cloves, finely grated
- 2 tsp finely grated ginger
- 2 spring onions, very finely chopped (about 25g)
- 1 1/2 tsp fine salt

### Turmeric tomatoes

- 400g sweet cherry tomatoes
- 15g ginger, peeled and julienned
- 15g coriander, stalks and leaves
- 3 garlic cloves, peeled
- 70ml olive oil
- 2 tsp maple syrup or honey
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 1/4 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp fine salt

### To serve

- 2 spring onions, finely sliced
- 5g coriander leaves
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 230°C fan (250°C conventional). Line a 23cm square baking tin or similar dish with baking paper.
2. Whisk all the rice-cake ingredients until the coconut milk is smooth. Pour into the tin and level the top.
3. Combine all the tomato ingredients in an ovenproof dish just large enough to hold them in one snug layer.
4. Bake both dishes for 30 minutes, with the tomatoes above the rice if they cannot share a shelf. The tomatoes should soften and char slightly; the rice should be cooked and golden.
5. Cover the tomatoes to keep warm. Let the rice rest for 20 minutes, then lift it with its paper onto a flat baking tray. Heat the grill to high.
6. Tear away any overhanging paper and grill the rice cake near the top of the oven for 5–8 minutes, until deeply golden and crisp.
7. Rest for 5 minutes, cut into squares and serve with the warm tomatoes, spring onions, coriander and lime wedges.

## My notes

- Check the rice packet: some brands need soaking overnight.
- Both parts can be made a day ahead. Pan-fry leftover rice-cake slices in a little oil.
